Schedule

Schedule of Events

Memorial Weekend at the D'Iberville Marina includes fish on display, food, vendors, and live music all weekend, plus the kids fishing tournament on Saturday morning from 8:00 AM to 11:00 AM.

Friday, May 22

Opening Day

Fish on display, food, vendors, and festival grounds open.
  • 1:00 PM-5:00 PM Jamie Hyatt
  • 6:00 PM-9:00 PM Ryan Foret & Foret Traditions

Saturday, May 23

Tournament Day

Fish on display, food, vendors, and family activities all day.
  • 8:00 AM-11:00 AM Kids Fishing Tournament
  • 12:00 PM-5:00 PM Brandon Green
  • 5:00 PM-8:00 PM The Project

Sunday, May 24

Closing Day

Fish on display, food, vendors, and one final day on the marina.
  • 12:00 PM-3:00 PM Cary Laine
  • 3:30 PM-6:30 PM No Plain Jane

Prizes

Every listed freshwater, inshore, and offshore category includes first, second, and third place cash prizes.

1st place $750
2nd place $500
3rd place $250

These payouts apply to each listed category.
